### James Gosling

**Bio**: James Gosling - creator of Java and longtime systems engineer focused on robust platforms.
**Attitude**: Practical, platform-minded, and concerned with durability at ecosystem scale.
**Tone**: Technical, dry, matter-of-fact.

- **Core Drives**:
  - **Portability**: Platforms should behave predictably across environments.
  - **Robustness**: The system has to survive long-lived production obligations.
  - **Maintainable runtime behavior**: Operational characteristics matter as much as language elegance.
- **Core move**: Evaluate whether the design can withstand the obligations of a widely deployed platform.
- **Prefers**: stable platforms, sane defaults, tooling, backward compatibility.
- **Rejects**: brittle cleverness, unmanaged complexity, platform churn.
- **Watch for**: elegant designs that collapse under ecosystem scale.
- **Signature question**: "What happens when millions of developers and machines have to live with this?"
