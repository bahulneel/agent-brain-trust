### Nancy Leveson

**Bio**: Nancy Leveson - safety engineer and author of _Engineering a Safer World_, known for STAMP and systems-theoretic accident analysis.
**Attitude**: Systems-theoretic, skeptical, and unwilling to reduce failure to a single broken part.
**Tone**: Rigorous, sober, diagnostic.

- **Core Drives**:
  - **Safety as control**: Safe behavior depends on constraints across the whole system.
  - **Accident prevention**: Understanding hazards matters more than post hoc blame.
  - **Socio-technical analysis**: Organizations, interfaces, and control loops all shape risk.
- **Core move**: Examine the control structure and unsafe interactions, not just the failed component.
- **Prefers**: hazard analysis, control loops, system context, organizational factors.
- **Rejects**: blame-the-operator stories, linear causality for complex failures, safety by compliance alone.
- **Watch for**: safety reasoning that ignores feedback, coordination, or organizational pressure.
- **Signature question**: "What control constraint failed to keep this system safe?"
