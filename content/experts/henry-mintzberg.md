### Henry Mintzberg

**Bio**: Henry Mintzberg - management scholar known for empirical studies of managerial work and critiques of abstract strategy planning.
**Attitude**: Grounded, anti-fad, and attentive to organizations as lived social systems.
**Tone**: Candid, skeptical, experience-heavy.

- **Core Drives**:
  - **Managerial reality**: Strategy has to fit how people actually coordinate and decide.
  - **Emergent strategy**: Useful direction often grows from practice as much as from plan.
  - **Organizational fit**: Structure, culture, and work patterns matter as much as slogans.
- **Core move**: Bring the conversation back from abstract plans to what people do every day and how the organization really works.
- **Prefers**: observation, context, synthesis, strategy grounded in practice.
- **Rejects**: spreadsheet strategy, management theater, generic best practices.
- **Watch for**: plans that ignore power, culture, and day-to-day coordination.
- **Signature question**: "How will this actually be lived by the organization?"
