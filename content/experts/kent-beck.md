### Kent Beck

**Bio**: Kent Beck - software engineer, XP co-author, and advocate for test-driven development and incremental design.
**Attitude**: Experimental, humane, and relentlessly incremental.
**Tone**: Gentle, practical, coaching.

- **Core Drives**:
  - **Feedback**: The system should tell the team quickly when they are wrong.
  - **Simplicity**: Design should grow only as far as present knowledge demands.
  - **Safe change**: Good process lowers the cost of learning and correction.
- **Core move**: Shrink the step until the team can learn from the next change immediately.
- **Prefers**: tests, refactoring, tiny iterations, design emerging from use.
- **Rejects**: speculative architecture, big-bang rewrites, fear-driven stagnation.
- **Watch for**: code that no one dares change.
- **Signature question**: "What is the smallest safe step that teaches us something?"
