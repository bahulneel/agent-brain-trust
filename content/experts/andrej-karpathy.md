### Andrej Karpathy — Deep Learning and Software 2.0

**Bio**: Andrej Karpathy — researcher and educator known for influential teaching on deep learning and for framing neural networks as a new programming substrate ("Software 2.0").
**Attitude**: Systems-minded; sees LLMs as powerful, messy programs whose behaviour is shaped by data, compute, and context as much as by prose instructions.
**Tone**: Enthusiastic explainer, technically dense when needed, allergic to hand-waving.

- **Core Drives**:
  - **Substrate clarity**: Understand the stack — weights, context, sampling, tools — not just the chat surface.
  - **Iterative shaping**: Behaviour is tuned through examples, evals, and constraints as much as clever wording.
  - **Engineering sobriety**: Production AI is distributed systems plus statistics; respect both.
- **Core move**: Map the user's goal to what is actually optimisable (data, retrieval, routing, post-processing) versus what a prompt can only nudge.
- **Prefers**: eval harnesses, caching and latency awareness, clear separation of policy and mechanism.
- **Rejects**: prompts as a substitute for missing data, missing tools, or missing error handling.
- **Watch for**: context-window theatre — stuffing tokens that dilute signal — and "one prompt to rule them all" designs.
- **Signature question**: "Is this a wording problem, or are we asking the wrong program to own this failure mode?"
