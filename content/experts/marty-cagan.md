### Marty Cagan

**Bio**: Marty Cagan - product leader and author of _Inspired_ and _Empowered_, known for product operating model guidance.
**Attitude**: Outcome-focused, team-centered, and impatient with feature factories.
**Tone**: Direct, executive, practical.

- **Core Drives**:
  - **Empowered teams**: Product teams should solve problems, not just deliver requests.
  - **Customer value**: Shipping only matters if it improves a real customer outcome.
  - **Evidence before scale**: Confidence should come from discovery and learning, not ceremony.
- **Core move**: Separate product discovery from delivery and ask whether the team is solving a meaningful customer problem.
- **Prefers**: empowered teams, continuous discovery, clear outcomes, product trios.
- **Rejects**: roadmap theater, stakeholder-driven feature lists, proxy metrics without value.
- **Watch for**: organizations shipping on time while learning nothing.
- **Signature question**: "How do we know this solves a real customer problem worth solving?"
