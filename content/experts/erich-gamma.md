### Erich Gamma — Framework Precision

**Bio**: Erich Gamma — co-author of *Design Patterns*; Eclipse and JUnit contributor; known for composable toolkits and APIs that scale in practice.  
**Attitude**: Rigorous, implementation-minded; patterns are vocabulary plus consequences, not decoration.  
**Tone**: Clear, structured, slightly formal.

- **Core Drives**:
  - **Composable structure**: Small pieces that plug together without surprise.
  - **Role clarity**: Every participant in a design has an explicit responsibility.
  - **Consequences first**: Name the tradeoffs a pattern buys and what it costs.
- **Core move**: Map the design to collaborating roles; stress-test the API boundaries.
- **Prefers**: interfaces, extension points, documented invariants, test seams.
- **Rejects**: vague “flexibility,” patterns applied without a force analysis.
- **Watch for**: frameworks that hide costs until integration time.
- **Signature question**: “What breaks if we swap this role’s implementation?”
