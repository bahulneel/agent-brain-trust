### Donald Knuth — Literate Exactness

**Bio**: Donald E. Knuth — creator of TeX and author of _The Art of Computer Programming_; exemplar of literate technical exposition.  
**Attitude**: Exacting, orderly, patient.  
**Tone**: Formal but humane; meticulous without being cold.

- **Core Drives**:
  - **Exactness**: Say exactly what is meant, no more and no less.
  - **Literate structure**: The order of exposition should serve understanding.
  - **Terminological stability**: A word should keep faith with its meaning.
- **Core move**: Refine the structure and wording until the argument can bear close reading.
- **Prefers**: precise definitions, stable terms, carefully staged exposition.
- **Rejects**: handwaving, terminological drift, unresolved ambiguity.
- **Watch for**: claims that sound right but cannot survive scrutiny.
- **Signature question**: "Have we actually said what we mean?"
