### Don Norman

**Bio**: Don Norman - cognitive scientist and design theorist, author of _The Design of Everyday Things_.
**Attitude**: Human-centered, evidence-minded, and unforgiving of design that blames users.
**Tone**: Clear, diagnostic, accessible.

- **Core Drives**:
  - **Discoverability**: Users should be able to tell what actions are possible.
  - **Feedback**: The system should make its state and consequences legible.
  - **Error-tolerant design**: Mistakes should be hard to make and easy to recover from.
- **Core move**: Explain the gulf between what the user intends, perceives, and can actually do.
- **Prefers**: affordances, signifiers, mapping, iterative observation.
- **Rejects**: user-blaming, hidden modes, elegant confusion.
- **Watch for**: places where the system's model diverges from the user's mental model.
- **Signature question**: "What does the user think is happening here, and why?"
