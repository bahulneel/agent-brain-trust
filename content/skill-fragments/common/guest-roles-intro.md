### Guest Roles

Guests serve in one of three roles. Multiple roles may be active in the same debate, and the same person may hold more than one role (e.g. Expert Witness later serving as Designated Challenger).

### Drafting discipline (mandatory — not optional)

**Cohort guest drafting is a required part of Cohort Construction**, not an embellishment you may skip for speed. **Do not** advance to Discovery (if applicable), Position, or any intra-cohort round until **every** cohort guest slot is filled with a complete persona. Treat omission of cohort guests as a **protocol violation**, not a shortcut.

**Strong presumption:** **Every** cohort **always** gets **exactly one** cohort guest — **no cohort, no topic, and no partition** exempts a slot. Count check: **2 cohorts ⇒ 2 guests; 3 cohorts ⇒ 3 guests.** There is **no** “optional third” or “this cohort doesn’t need one.”

The Moderator **must** complete a full **Guest Persona Format** for every guest slot **before** the phase that needs that guest. Do not leave a role vacant, use placeholders, or defer naming until mid-round. **Every slot the protocol opens must be filled** with a named, real figure and a stated rationale.

**To find the right expert for a slot**, call skill **`draft-experts`** **once per cohort** with that cohort’s **named** blind spot / tension friction / gap — not a generic session query shared copy-pasted across cohorts unless the gaps truly coincide (rare). Use returned expert ids; **do not** improvise from general knowledge or treat a roster member as the cohort guest.

- **Cohort Guest** — **One slot per cohort** (2 or 3 total, matching cohort count). Run **`draft-experts`** per cohort for its **distinct** productive friction on an axis it straddles; publish **full** personas in **Justify** **before** any intra-cohort debate. **Each cohort’s guest must be a different named person** — the **same** figure **cannot** be cohort guest for two cohorts in one session.
- **Expert Witness** — **At most one** for the whole debate. Use **`draft-experts`** **before Readings** when the **Expert Witness** fragment’s trigger or **strong presumption** applies; **Discovery** (Q&A) still runs **before Position** when this slot is open. **Do not open** the slot only when a **Valid narrow exception** there is satisfied and recorded. If the gap emerges only at **Tension Axis Analysis**, draft the witness there **before Discovery** (rare).
- **Designated Challenger** — **At most one.** **Primary:** **before Readings** when primary trigger or **strong presumption** applies (**operational** stakes count). **Fallback:** Rebuttal **deadlock** if none was drafted early → draft **before Refine / Synthesis**. Run **`draft-experts`**. **Mandatory if** a trigger fires; may match the Expert Witness if one was drafted.

### Pre-readings waivers (high bar — do not rationalise away guests)

Skipping **Expert Witness** or **Designated Challenger** with hand‑waving counts as **protocol drift**. The following **do not** justify closing a slot **by themselves**:

- Claiming the panel can handle the topic **“tactically”** or **because the work is in-repo** — concrete systems, RPC/peers/strata, sequencing, and verification still implicate **field practice, failure modes, and contestable orderings** the roster does not own by default.
- **“No load‑bearing gap”** without stating **which** established roster mandate already exhausts the needed authority and why **no** external witness would change first‑round readings.
- **“No Designated Challenger”** because stakes are **operational**, **about verification**, or **not ideological** — those are **primary** challenger stakes (ordering, falsification, risk, incompatible goods), not exceptions.

To leave a pre‑readings slot **closed**, the Moderator must record a **specific, checkable** reason that meets the **Valid narrow exceptions** in each role fragment — not a rhetorical summary.

### Cohort guest waivers (there are none — only invalid excuses)

A cohort guest slot **cannot** be closed. The following **do not** waive drafting:

- **“No blind spot”** / **“cohorts are already balanced”** — name the friction each guest sharpens anyway; **draft-experts** still runs per cohort.
- **“Topic is narrow”**, **“session is short”**, or **token / length** pressure.
- **“We’ll add the guest in debate”**, **TBD**, **same name as another cohort’s guest**, or **re-using** one **`draft-experts`** result for multiple cohorts without **per-cohort** gap text and justification.
- Treating a **roster member** as the cohort guest — cohort guests are **additional** drafted figures, not the panel relabelled.

If the user or writer rejects a guest, run **`draft-experts`** again for the same gap and replace with another full persona before continuing — the slot stays open until acceptably filled.
