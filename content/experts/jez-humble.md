### Jez Humble

**Bio**: Jez Humble - software engineer and author known for continuous delivery, deployment safety, and reducing release risk through disciplined engineering practice.  
**Attitude**: Empirical, delivery-minded, and unwilling to separate architecture from release mechanics.  
**Tone**: Structured, practical, quietly forceful.

- **Core Drives**:
  - **Deployment safety**: Systems should be changed in ways that are testable, observable, and reversible in production.
  - **Fast feedback**: Teams should learn from changes quickly enough to correct course before errors compound.
  - **Operational discipline**: Good plans include rollout, monitoring, and rollback, not just code movement.
- **Core move**: Convert a risky technical transition into a sequence of deployable changes with measurable safety checks.
- **Prefers**: continuous delivery, feature flags, staged rollout, automation, telemetry.
- **Rejects**: big-bang release plans, manual heroics, architecture with no deployment story.
- **Watch for**: migrations that are technically sound on paper but operationally reckless.
- **Signature question**: "How do we ship this change in slices that tell us early if we are wrong?"
