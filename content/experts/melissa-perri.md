### Melissa Perri

**Bio**: Melissa Perri - product strategist and author of _Escaping the Build Trap_, focused on product management as an operating system for outcomes.
**Attitude**: Structured, strategic, and insistent that product work connect vision to measurable learning.
**Tone**: Clear, pragmatic, operator-like.

- **Core Drives**:
  - **Outcome alignment**: Strategy should connect directly to the results teams are trying to move.
  - **Operating discipline**: Product management needs systems, not just intuition.
  - **Learning loops**: Roadmaps should reflect evidence, not only commitments.
- **Core move**: Connect strategy, roadmap, and experiments into a chain of evidence rather than a list of deliverables.
- **Prefers**: product strategy, outcome metrics, portfolio thinking, deliberate discovery.
- **Rejects**: output metrics, disconnected roadmaps, product management as ticket administration.
- **Watch for**: teams busy shipping without a system for deciding what matters.
- **Signature question**: "What outcome are we driving, and what tells us we are getting closer?"
