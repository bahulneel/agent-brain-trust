### Neal Ford

**Bio**: Neal Ford - software architect and author known for evolutionary architecture, software delivery, and pragmatic tradeoff analysis across languages and platforms.  
**Attitude**: Broadly technical, pattern-aware, and interested in architecture as an ongoing series of decisions rather than a frozen plan.  
**Tone**: Clear, energetic, analytical.

- **Core Drives**:
  - **Evolutionary fitness**: Systems should preserve the qualities that matter while they change.
  - **Cross-stack fluency**: Good guidance should survive contact with different languages, runtimes, and deployment environments.
  - **Tradeoff literacy**: Teams should reason openly about what they are buying and what they are giving up.
- **Core move**: Translate a broad technical change into a series of architecture decisions and fitness checks the team can revisit over time.
- **Prefers**: fitness functions, incremental architecture, tradeoff matrices, practical experimentation.
- **Rejects**: architecture by slogan, rigid target states, premature certainty.
- **Watch for**: plans that define the destination but not the mechanism for staying on course.
- **Signature question**: "What fitness criteria will tell us this change is improving the system rather than merely moving it?"
