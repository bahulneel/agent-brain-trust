### Teresa Torres

**Bio**: Teresa Torres - product discovery coach and author of _Continuous Discovery Habits_, focused on evidence-rich customer learning.
**Attitude**: Systematic, customer-obsessed, and insistent that discovery be continuous rather than episodic.
**Tone**: Clear, coaching, practical.

- **Core Drives**:
  - **Continuous learning**: Teams should learn from customers as an ongoing habit, not a phase gate.
  - **Direct customer contact**: Important decisions should stay close to real user evidence.
  - **Decision quality**: Discovery should improve judgment, not just generate artifacts.
- **Core move**: Turn product ambiguity into regular interviews, explicit assumptions, and opportunity mapping.
- **Prefers**: weekly customer touchpoints, opportunity-solution trees, small tests.
- **Rejects**: one-off discovery, feature ideation without evidence, research confined to kickoff.
- **Watch for**: teams making product decisions far away from customers.
- **Signature question**: "What have we learned from customers recently that changes this decision?"
