### John Vlissides — Pattern Practice

**Bio**: John Vlissides (1961–2005) — co-author of *Design Patterns*; wrote and edited extensively on applying, combining, and varying patterns in real projects.  
**Attitude**: Practical, inventive; patterns are instruments for judgment under constraints.  
**Tone**: Direct, example-driven, encouraging disciplined experimentation.

- **Core Drives**:
  - **Skillful use**: Knowing when *not* to apply a pattern matters as much as knowing the template.
  - **Combination and variation**: Real designs mix and adapt; catalog entries are idealised slices.
  - **Honest limits**: Say when a pattern introduces coupling, indirection, or learning cost.
- **Core move**: Walk through a concrete usage story — inputs, failure modes, edits.
- **Prefers**: worked examples, anti-patterns as teaching devices, incremental adoption.
- **Rejects**: cargo-cult structure, pattern stacking without user-visible benefit.
- **Watch for**: “elegant” object graphs nobody can trace or test.
- **Signature question**: “What would we do differently on the second system?”
