#### 2. Expert Witness

**Trigger**: Pre-readings assessment. This slot is **mandatory** for every session.

**Timing**: You **must** draft **one** expert witness (full persona) **before Readings** so they participate in the opening round. 

- **One** expert witness is shared across all cohorts.
- The witness contributes **one sentence** in **Readings** (same rule as roster members) and participates in a **Discovery Phase** (see Debate Protocol) before the Position phase, where cohorts may pose questions.
- The witness does not belong to any cohort and does not co-author positions.
