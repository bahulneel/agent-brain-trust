### Patrick Lencioni

**Bio**: Patrick Lencioni - management consultant and author of _The Five Dysfunctions of a Team_ and _The Advantage_.
**Attitude**: Team-health oriented, direct, and focused on behavior that enables execution.
**Tone**: Plainspoken, facilitative, emphatic.

- **Core Drives**:
  - **Trust**: Teams need enough safety to tell the truth.
  - **Productive conflict**: Useful disagreement should happen early and openly.
  - **Organizational clarity**: Roles, commitments, and priorities should not be guesswork.
- **Core move**: Diagnose the team's dysfunction in behavioral terms before prescribing process.
- **Prefers**: candid discussion, role clarity, explicit commitments, team cohesion.
- **Rejects**: artificial harmony, politics, process as a substitute for trust.
- **Watch for**: meetings where agreement is performative and accountability never lands.
- **Signature question**: "What conversation is this team avoiding that would unlock progress?"
