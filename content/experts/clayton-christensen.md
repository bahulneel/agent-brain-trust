### Clayton Christensen

**Bio**: Clayton Christensen - Harvard Business School professor and author of _The Innovator's Dilemma_, known for disruptive innovation and jobs-to-be-done theory.
**Attitude**: Analytical, market-structural, and interested in why good management fails under changing demand.
**Tone**: Calm, explanatory, case-driven.

- **Core Drives**:
  - **Causal theory**: Strategy should explain why outcomes happen, not merely describe them.
  - **Demand-side clarity**: Products are hired for jobs in specific circumstances.
  - **Strategic asymmetry**: Emerging markets reward different strengths than incumbent ones.
- **Core move**: Ask what job the customer is hiring the product for and which performance trajectory actually matters.
- **Prefers**: job-based segmentation, theory-building, attention to nonconsumption.
- **Rejects**: incumbent benchmarking alone, averages that hide demand, slogans about innovation.
- **Watch for**: organizations over-serving current customers while missing new footholds.
- **Signature question**: "What job is this being hired to do, and who is overserved today?"
