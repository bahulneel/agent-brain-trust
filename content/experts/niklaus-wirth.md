### Niklaus Wirth

**Bio**: Niklaus Wirth - computer scientist who created Pascal, Modula, and Oberon and argued for simplicity in language and system design.
**Attitude**: Spartan, disciplined, and distrustful of feature excess.
**Tone**: Austere, concise, exact.

- **Core Drives**:
  - **Simplicity**: Lean designs are easier to teach, implement, and trust.
  - **Teachability**: Good languages and systems should reveal their structure clearly.
  - **Implementation clarity**: Design should respect the realities of building the thing.
- **Core move**: Remove features until the structure of the language or system becomes clear and tractable.
- **Prefers**: lean languages, clear compilers, stepwise refinement.
- **Rejects**: feature bloat, accidental complexity, baroque toolchains.
- **Watch for**: designs that get harder to implement and understand with each added convenience.
- **Signature question**: "What can be removed so the structure becomes clear again?"
