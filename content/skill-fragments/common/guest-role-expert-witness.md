#### 2. Expert Witness

**Trigger**: From the **user’s brief**, **artifact** (code, design notes, RPC/peer/stratum descriptions, etc.), or **Moderator pre‑readings assessment** — a **load‑bearing** need for authority **outside** the roster’s implicit consensus: semantics of concrete substrates, interoperability and failure behaviour, operational truth, precedent from practice, or specialist doctrine **no** cohort is chartered to settle as oracle.

**Strong presumption (open the slot)**: The brief depends on **how** named or implied **systems pieces** behave together (networked or cross‑boundary calls, peer roles, layers, lifecycle, correctness under concurrency, rollout semantics) **and** getting it wrong would change recommendations. **Proximity to the codebase does not waive this** — “in‑repo” work is exactly where **witnessable** norms and failure modes matter.

**Valid narrow exception (close the slot)**: The ask is purely conceptual with **no** commitment to concrete behaviour, **or** a roster member’s mandate **explicitly** covers the same specialty and the Moderator names them as sufficient authority — not “we can reason tactically.”

The same gap may surface during **Tension Axis Analysis** if it was missed earlier; see Cohort Construction.

**Timing**: If this trigger applies, you **must** draft **one** expert witness (full persona) **before Readings** so they participate in the opening round. If no collective gap is identified under the bar above, **do not** open this slot — skip Discovery as well.

- **One** expert witness is shared across all cohorts.
- The witness contributes **one sentence** in **Readings** when drafted in pre-readings (same rule as roster members) and participates in a **Discovery Phase** (see Debate Protocol) before the Position phase, where cohorts may pose questions. If the witness is drafted only **before Discovery** because the gap emerged late, they **do not** retroactively join Readings; they enter at Discovery.
- The witness does not belong to any cohort and does not co-author positions.
