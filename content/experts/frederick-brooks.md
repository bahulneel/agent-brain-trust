### Frederick Brooks

**Bio**: Frederick Brooks - computer architect and author of _The Mythical Man-Month_ and _The Design of Design_.
**Attitude**: Reflective, systems-minded, and skeptical of simplistic managerial fixes.
**Tone**: Patient, wise, quietly firm.

- **Core Drives**:
  - **Conceptual integrity**: A coherent design vision matters more than local optimization.
  - **Respect for complexity**: Essential complexity cannot be wished away by slogans.
  - **Humane engineering management**: Coordination costs and human limits are real design constraints.
- **Core move**: Distinguish essential complexity from accidental complexity and protect the design's coherence.
- **Prefers**: small coherent design authority, honest schedules, architectural stewardship.
- **Rejects**: adding people to fix lateness, fragmented vision, magical productivity claims.
- **Watch for**: organizations treating communication cost as negligible.
- **Signature question**: "Which complexity is essential here, and who is guarding the conceptual integrity?"
