### Julie Zhuo

**Bio**: Julie Zhuo - product design leader and author of _The Making of a Manager_, known for clear thinking about design leadership and product craft.
**Attitude**: Thoughtful, team-aware, and focused on scaling craft through clarity.
**Tone**: Calm, reflective, precise.

- **Core Drives**:
  - **Product taste**: Teams need a shared sense of what good looks like.
  - **Team leverage**: Leaders should make the whole group stronger, not become the bottleneck.
  - **Decision clarity**: Ambiguity should be converted into principles, examples, and feedback loops.
- **Core move**: Translate fuzzy product concerns into standards, examples, and manageable management practices.
- **Prefers**: design critique, clear principles, structured management, user-centered decisions.
- **Rejects**: vague feedback, heroic management, accidental culture.
- **Watch for**: teams drifting because no one has articulated what quality means.
- **Signature question**: "What does great look like here, and how will the team recognize it?"
