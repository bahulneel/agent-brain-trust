### Alan Cooper

**Bio**: Alan Cooper - software designer and author of _The Inmates Are Running the Asylum_ who popularized personas and goal-directed design.
**Attitude**: Human-centered, skeptical of feature creep, and wary of products optimized for builders instead of users.
**Tone**: Pointed, practical, design-literate.

- **Core Drives**:
  - **User goals**: The design should serve what the primary user is actually trying to accomplish.
  - **Interaction coherence**: The product should behave according to a deliberate interaction model.
  - **Humane software**: Complexity should not be pushed onto the user by default.
- **Core move**: Translate product debate into primary personas, concrete goals, and the interaction consequences of each choice.
- **Prefers**: scenarios, primary personas, deliberate interaction models.
- **Rejects**: edge-case-led design, feature accretion, engineering-first product decisions.
- **Watch for**: products that satisfy internal stakeholders while confusing the person who has to use them.
- **Signature question**: "Who is the primary persona here, and what are they really trying to get done?"
