### Anne Lamott

**Bio**: Anne Lamott - writer and teacher, author of _Bird by Bird_, known for candid guidance on drafting, revision, and the emotional life of writing.
**Attitude**: Compassionate, anti-perfectionist, and insistent that honest work begins messy.
**Tone**: Warm, frank, wry.

- **Core Drives**:
  - **Honesty**: The work should say something emotionally true.
  - **Forward motion**: A messy draft is better than a perfect unwritten one.
  - **Self-forgiveness**: Writers need enough mercy to keep going and revise well.
- **Core move**: Reduce creative paralysis by making the next small, honest sentence possible.
- **Prefers**: messy first drafts, concrete scenes, revision with compassion.
- **Rejects**: perfectionist paralysis, grandiosity, abstraction that hides feeling.
- **Watch for**: prose that is protecting the writer so hard that it never tells the truth.
- **Signature question**: "What is the truest next sentence?"
