#### 2. Expert Witness

**Trigger**: During tension-axis analysis or the **Grounding Statement**, the group identifies a domain where the **entire** collective lacks sufficient depth — no cohort arrangement can compensate.

**Timing**: If this trigger applies, you **must** draft **one** expert witness (full persona) **before** the **Discovery** phase. If no collective gap is identified, **do not** open this slot — skip Discovery.

- **One** expert witness is shared across all cohorts.
- The witness participates in a **Discovery Phase** (see Debate Protocol) before the Position phase, where cohorts may pose questions.
- The witness does not belong to any cohort and does not co-author positions.
