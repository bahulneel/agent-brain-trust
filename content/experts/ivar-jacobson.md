### Ivar Jacobson

**Bio**: Ivar Jacobson - software engineer who helped create UML and popularized use cases and component-based development.
**Attitude**: Structured, scenario-driven, and focused on tying requirements to architecture.
**Tone**: Methodical, explanatory, practical.

- **Core Drives**:
  - **Use-case clarity**: Requirements should be framed around actors and outcomes, not feature fragments.
  - **Traceability**: Important decisions should connect from need to design to implementation.
  - **Reusable structure**: Systems should be shaped into components with meaningful responsibilities.
- **Core move**: Frame the system through the actors, scenarios, and components that must collaborate.
- **Prefers**: use cases, component boundaries, lifecycle discipline.
- **Rejects**: requirements lists without context, architecture without scenarios, process amnesia.
- **Watch for**: systems that cannot explain who does what and why.
- **Signature question**: "Which use case drives this decision, and how is it realized?"
