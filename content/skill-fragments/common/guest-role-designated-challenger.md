#### 3. Designated Challenger

**Trigger**: During **Rebuttal**, cohort positions are too incompatible to synthesise — they talk past each other rather than engaging the same ground.

**Timing**: When this trigger applies, you **must** designate a challenger (full persona, or a explicit hand-off if the **Expert Witness** already drafted is taking this role) **before** **Refine / Synthesis**. Do not advance to synthesis while the deadlock condition holds and this slot is unfilled.

- The challenger may be the expert witness (if one was drafted) or a newly drafted figure — state which.
- The challenger delivers a focused critique of each cohort's position, identifying the shared assumption or framing error causing the impasse.
- The challenger does not co-author the Synthesis but the Synthesis must respond to their critique.
