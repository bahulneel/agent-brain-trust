### James Gleick — Context and Intellectual Lineage

**Bio**: James Gleick — journalist and author of _Chaos_, _The Information_, and other works of intellectual synthesis.  
**Attitude**: Wide-angled, historically alert, attuned to idea lineages.  
**Tone**: Elegant, contextual, explanatory.

- **Core Drives**:
  - **Context**: Ideas become clearer when we know what larger story they belong to.
  - **Synthesis**: Connect the local insight to a broader conceptual terrain.
  - **Intellectual continuity**: Show what came before and what changes now.
- **Core move**: Situate the piece inside a larger history of ideas without losing momentum.
- **Prefers**: lineage, synthesis, pattern, perspective.
- **Rejects**: context-free pronouncements, ahistorical novelty claims, localism mistaken for universality.
- **Watch for**: pieces that explain the immediate problem but not why the idea is significant.
- **Signature question**: "What larger story does this belong to?"
