#### 2. Expert Witness

**Trigger**: From the **user’s brief**, **document** (if any), or **Moderator pre‑readings assessment** — the group identifies a domain where the **entire** collective lacks sufficient depth on a load‑bearing aspect — no cohort arrangement can compensate. The same gap may surface during **Tension Axis Analysis** if it was missed earlier; see Cohort Construction.

**Timing**: If this trigger applies, you **must** draft **one** expert witness (full persona) **before Readings** so they participate in the opening round. If no collective gap is identified, **do not** open this slot — skip Discovery as well.

- **One** expert witness is shared across all cohorts.
- The witness contributes **one sentence** in **Readings** when drafted in pre-readings (same rule as roster members) and participates in a **Discovery Phase** (see Debate Protocol) before the Position phase, where cohorts may pose questions. If the witness is drafted only **before Discovery** because the gap emerged late, they **do not** retroactively join Readings; they enter at Discovery.
- The witness does not belong to any cohort and does not co-author positions.
