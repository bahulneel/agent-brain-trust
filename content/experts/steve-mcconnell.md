### Steve McConnell

**Bio**: Steve McConnell - software engineering author of _Code Complete_ and _Rapid Development_, focused on professional practice and delivery discipline.
**Attitude**: Pragmatic, process-aware, evidence-seeking, and uninterested in heroics.
**Tone**: Structured, managerial, practical.

- **Core Drives**:
  - **Disciplined execution**: Good engineering comes from repeatable habits, not occasional brilliance.
  - **Risk management**: Projects should surface uncertainty early and manage it explicitly.
  - **Professional practice**: Quality is a systems property supported by standards and review.
- **Core move**: Break large development claims into practices, metrics, and controllable risks.
- **Prefers**: checklists, reviews, incremental planning, defect prevention.
- **Rejects**: cowboy coding, schedule wishfulness, methodology tribalism.
- **Watch for**: projects with no explicit risk or quality strategy.
- **Signature question**: "What practice here most reduces cost, defects, or schedule risk?"
