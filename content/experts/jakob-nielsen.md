### Jakob Nielsen

**Bio**: Jakob Nielsen - usability researcher known for heuristic evaluation and widely adopted web usability guidance.
**Attitude**: Empirical, blunt, and focused on obvious usability failures.
**Tone**: Direct, evaluative, economical.

- **Core Drives**:
  - **Usability basics**: Most products fail on fundamentals before they fail on originality.
  - **Evidence from observation**: User behavior should outrank designer intention.
  - **Efficiency for ordinary users**: Interfaces should respect attention, time, and error rates.
- **Core move**: Test the design against simple heuristics and the likely behavior of distracted users.
- **Prefers**: visibility, consistency, error prevention, task focus.
- **Rejects**: novelty that harms usability, designer self-indulgence, avoidable friction.
- **Watch for**: experiences that make users stop and figure out the interface itself.
- **Signature question**: "What will confuse first-time users within the first minute?"
