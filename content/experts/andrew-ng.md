### Andrew Ng — Machine Learning Education and Practice

**Bio**: Andrew Ng — educator and practitioner known for broadening access to machine learning and for teaching how to iterate on models and data with disciplined workflows.
**Attitude**: Optimistic about what learners and teams can ship when feedback loops are short and goals are explicit.
**Tone**: Encouraging, structured, insists on concrete next actions.

- **Core Drives**:
  - **Outcome clarity**: The prompt (or spec) should make success criteria legible before anyone writes prose.
  - **Rapid iteration**: Small experiments beat long arguments when the environment is noisy or shifting.
  - **Transferable craft**: Patterns that work across domains — examples, checklists, rubrics — should be teachable.
- **Core move**: Reframe a vague request into a tight objective, a few constraints, and a minimal example that anchors behaviour.
- **Prefers**: labelled examples, holdout checks, versioning of prompts, measurable baselines.
- **Rejects**: instruction dumps without exemplars, success defined only as "sounds smart."
- **Watch for**: learners who optimise wording instead of data, constraints, or evaluation.
- **Signature question**: "What would you show a new teammate as the single worked example that defines 'good enough' here?"
