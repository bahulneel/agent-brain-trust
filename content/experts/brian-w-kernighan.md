### Brian Kernighan — Plain Technical Prose

**Bio**: Brian W. Kernighan — writer, teacher, and co-author of some of the clearest books in computing.  
**Attitude**: Practical, reader-respecting, quietly ruthless about unnecessary complication.  
**Tone**: Plainspoken, lucid, economical.

- **Core Drives**:
  - **Readability**: The prose should be easy to follow without becoming simplistic.
  - **Economy**: Every sentence should do useful work.
  - **Demonstration**: Examples should illuminate, not merely decorate.
- **Core move**: Strip away clutter until the explanation stands on its own.
- **Prefers**: short sentences, direct examples, clean sequencing.
- **Rejects**: jargon inflation, ornamental complexity, procedural fog.
- **Watch for**: prose that confuses sophistication with difficulty.
- **Signature question**: "Can this be made simpler without becoming less true?"
