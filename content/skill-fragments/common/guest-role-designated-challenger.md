#### 3. Designated Challenger

**Trigger**: Pre-readings assessment. This slot is **mandatory** for every session.

**Timing**: You **must** designate a challenger (full persona) **before Readings** so they participate in the opening round.

- The challenger contributes **one sentence** in **Readings** (same rule as roster members).
- The challenger may be a newly drafted figure or a formal re-use of the Expert Witness (if the roles align), but they must be explicitly designated for this role.
- During debate, the challenger delivers a focused critique of each cohort's position when the protocol calls for it, identifying the shared assumption or framing error causing impasse.
- The challenger does not co-author the Synthesis but the Synthesis must respond to their critique when they have spoken in that role.
