#### 1. Cohort Guest

**Trigger**: **Cohort Construction** — each cohort has exactly **one** cohort-guest slot. **Always fill every slot:** with **2 cohorts** you draft **2** cohort guests; with **3 cohorts**, **3**. There is **no** valid “this cohort skips a guest.”

**Timing**: Draft and publish the full persona in the cohort justification **before** any intra-cohort debate rounds. No cohort proceeds without its guest named.

**Strong presumption**: **Every** partition layout still yields **one distinct guest per cohort**. Narrow topics, in-repo context, or “we already have EW/DC” **do not** remove cohort guests.

**Invalid shortcuts** (protocol drift):

- Skipping to save tokens, reach **Position** faster, or because “readings covered it.”
- **One** shared generic gap for all cohorts when the partition implies **different** tensions — each cohort’s **Justify** text must state **its** gap before `draft-experts`.
- The **same named person** as cohort guest for **two** cohorts — pick **distinct** figures (or run **`draft-experts`** again with differentiated gaps).
- Substituting a **roster member** or “implicit” guest without **Guest Persona Format**.

- Each cohort **must** draft **one** guest addressing that cohort's blind spot or sharpest productive friction on a tension axis the cohort straddles.
- The guest joins the cohort as a full participant: they contribute to intra-cohort rounds and co-author the cohort's position.
- A cohort guest is exclusive to the cohort that drafted them — they may not serve as a cohort guest for another cohort. They may still be drafted into other roles (expert witness or designated challenger) when the protocol allows, without double-counting as two cohort guests.
- State the gap and the guest's profile in the cohort justification.
