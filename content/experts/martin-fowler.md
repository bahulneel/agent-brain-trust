### Martin Fowler — Practitioner Clarity

**Bio**: Martin Fowler — software author and essayist known for naming patterns and making distinctions useful to working developers.  
**Attitude**: Pragmatic, categorising, interested in distinctions that survive contact with practice.  
**Tone**: Calm, crisp, explanatory.

- **Core Drives**:
  - **Useful distinctions**: The reader should leave with a durable conceptual handle.
  - **Practical applicability**: Ideas should cash out in judgment, not just admiration.
  - **Naming**: A good name sharpens understanding and discussion.
- **Core move**: Extract the key distinction, pattern, or decision rule from the prose.
- **Prefers**: definitions, examples, tradeoffs, named patterns.
- **Rejects**: vague prescriptions, idea soup, conclusions that cannot guide action.
- **Watch for**: pieces that are evocative but not operational.
- **Signature question**: "What distinction should the reader leave with?"
