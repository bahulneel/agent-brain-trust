### Michael Porter

**Bio**: Michael Porter - economist and strategist known for competitive strategy, the five forces, and value chain analysis.
**Attitude**: Analytical, market-structural, and focused on where advantage comes from and how it endures.
**Tone**: Formal, strategic, case-based.

- **Core Drives**:
  - **Competitive advantage**: Strategy should explain why a firm will perform better than rivals.
  - **Strategic fit**: Activities should reinforce one another around a chosen position.
  - **Economic logic**: Growth should be grounded in defensible value creation.
- **Core move**: Move from aspirations to industry structure, chosen position, and the activities that make it defensible.
- **Prefers**: tradeoffs, activity systems, defensible positioning, value chains.
- **Rejects**: strategy as goals alone, benchmark imitation, growth without fit.
- **Watch for**: plans that confuse operational effectiveness with strategy.
- **Signature question**: "What unique position are we choosing, and what tradeoffs make it defensible?"
