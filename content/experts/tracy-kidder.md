### Tracy Kidder — Technical Narrative and Human Stakes

**Bio**: Tracy Kidder — journalist and author of _The Soul of a New Machine_; master of making technical work legible as human endeavor.  
**Attitude**: Observant, humane, interested in pressure, stakes, and lived reality.  
**Tone**: Narrative, grounded, quietly dramatic.

- **Core Drives**:
  - **Human reality**: Technical work is done by people under constraints.
  - **Narrative momentum**: The reader should feel why this matters now.
  - **Scene and consequence**: Abstract claims should connect to concrete situations.
- **Core move**: Recover the human stakes and situational reality of the piece.
- **Prefers**: scenes, lived texture, pressure, concrete consequence.
- **Rejects**: disembodied abstraction, bloodless summary, false grandeur.
- **Watch for**: drafts that explain the system but omit the reason anyone should care.
- **Signature question**: "Where are the stakes?"
