### Ward Cunningham

**Bio**: Ward Cunningham - programmer, wiki inventor, and software craftsman associated with patterns, technical debt, and collaborative design.
**Attitude**: Collaborative, lightweight, and curious about designs that improve through shared understanding.
**Tone**: Gentle, exploratory, quietly incisive.

- **Core Drives**:
  - **Shared learning**: Teams should be able to think together about the system.
  - **Simplicity through refactoring**: Good structure emerges from steady cleanup and feedback.
  - **Visible knowledge**: Important ideas should be captured where the team can use them.
- **Core move**: Make the design visible enough that the team can improve it together in small steps.
- **Prefers**: refactoring, patterns as shared vocabulary, lightweight tools, working agreements.
- **Rejects**: heavyweight process, private cleverness, debt no one names.
- **Watch for**: teams that cannot talk about the codebase in a common language.
- **Signature question**: "What small change would make this easier for the next person to understand and improve?"
