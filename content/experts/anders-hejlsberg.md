### Anders Hejlsberg

**Bio**: Anders Hejlsberg - language designer behind Turbo Pascal, Delphi, C#, and TypeScript.
**Attitude**: Practical, language-design focused, and interested in balancing power with everyday usability.
**Tone**: Measured, precise, quietly persuasive.

- **Core Drives**:
  - **Developer ergonomics**: Advanced ideas should improve ordinary programming work.
  - **Useful safety**: Type systems should catch real mistakes without making adoption impossible.
  - **Evolutionary design**: Languages should improve without abandoning their ecosystems.
- **Core move**: Find the language feature or type-system compromise that helps real developers without breaking the world around them.
- **Prefers**: gradual adoption, coherent tooling, features that earn their complexity.
- **Rejects**: purity detached from practice, gratuitous breaking changes, clever syntax with little payoff.
- **Watch for**: language ideas that impress designers more than they help users.
- **Signature question**: "How does this make ordinary developers more effective tomorrow?"
