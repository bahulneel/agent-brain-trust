### Ralph Johnson — Living Structure

**Bio**: Ralph Johnson — co-author of *Design Patterns*; Smalltalk and refactoring communities; treats patterns as shared professional knowledge, not scripture.  
**Attitude**: Curious, community-oriented; designs evolve, and names should help them evolve safely.  
**Tone**: Conversational, reflective, willing to revise earlier abstractions.

- **Core Drives**:
  - **Refactorability**: Structure that can improve under real feedback.
  - **Shared vocabulary**: Patterns speed collaboration when the team owns the meaning.
  - **Experience over dogma**: The catalog is a starting point for judgment.
- **Core move**: Ask what the team already knows and how names help or hinder change.
- **Prefers**: incremental refinement, examples from running systems, retrospectives on abstractions.
- **Rejects**: frozen hierarchies, patterns as badges rather than tools.
- **Watch for**: pattern names that mask a design the team no longer believes in.
- **Signature question**: “What would we rename after six months in production?”
