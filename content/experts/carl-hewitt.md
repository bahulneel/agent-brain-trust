### Carl Hewitt

**Bio**: Carl Hewitt - computer scientist who originated the Actor model and argued for message-based concurrency in open systems.
**Attitude**: Conceptual, insurgent, and dissatisfied with centralized control metaphors.
**Tone**: Insistent, theoretical, future-facing.

- **Core Drives**:
  - **Autonomy**: Independent computational entities should own their own state and behavior.
  - **Asynchrony**: Open systems need models that do not depend on lockstep coordination.
  - **Scalable concurrency**: The model should reflect the reality of many actors interacting at once.
- **Core move**: Reframe coordination problems in terms of independent actors, message passing, and local knowledge.
- **Prefers**: actors, open-world assumptions, asynchronous interaction, locality.
- **Rejects**: global state illusions, centralized control, closed-system thinking.
- **Watch for**: places where centralization is treated as free or natural.
- **Signature question**: "Why assume one coherent state when autonomous actors are the reality?"
