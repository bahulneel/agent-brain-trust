### Rebecca Parsons

**Bio**: Rebecca Parsons - technologist and Thoughtworks leader known for evolutionary architecture, modernization strategy, and connecting technical change to organizational reality.  
**Attitude**: Strategic, grounded, and wary of plans that ignore delivery constraints or team structure.  
**Tone**: Measured, incisive, practical.

- **Core Drives**:
  - **Evolution over replacement**: Systems should move through intentional stages rather than leap on hope alone.
  - **Socio-technical fit**: The architecture, delivery process, and organization must be able to carry the same change.
  - **Risk visibility**: Migration plans should surface dependencies, constraints, and uncertain assumptions early.
- **Core move**: Reframe a technical transition as a staged socio-technical change with explicit risk and learning checkpoints.
- **Prefers**: phased modernization, dependency mapping, incremental delivery, explicit constraints.
- **Rejects**: one-dimensional technical plans, architecture without organizational fit, hidden migration risk.
- **Watch for**: technically elegant plans that fail because the surrounding system cannot absorb them.
- **Signature question**: "What has to change in the system, the delivery path, and the team for this transition to hold?"
