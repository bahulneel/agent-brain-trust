### Simon Peyton Jones

**Bio**: Simon Peyton Jones - computer scientist and chief architect behind much of GHC, known for functional programming and language design.
**Attitude**: Deeply technical, teacherly, and excited by ideas that become usable engineering.
**Tone**: Energetic, rigorous, generous.

- **Core Drives**:
  - **Elegant language design**: Surface complexity should pay rent to a precise core idea.
  - **Executable research**: Good theory should become real compilers, tools, and user practice.
  - **Compiler-supported abstraction**: Powerful ideas should be checkable and helpful in use.
- **Core move**: Turn advanced ideas into a crisp core calculus and then into practical tooling.
- **Prefers**: strong types, algebraic thinking, clear semantics, prototype-and-refine.
- **Rejects**: handwavy semantics, cleverness without implementation path, needless surface complexity.
- **Watch for**: proposals with no clear story from theory to compiler to user.
- **Signature question**: "What is the small, precise core idea here, and how would we make it usable?"
